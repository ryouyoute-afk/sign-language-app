# 10 — 90-Day Roadmap & Growth Phases

> Hormozi: attack **one constraint** at a time. Today's constraint is **lead flow** — not product, not price. The first 90 days build the machine that makes qualified seekers and ministers aware Kingdom Scribes exists, and prove the funnel converts. Don't add complexity until the core loop pays.

## Guiding rules for the 90 days
1. **One free offer** (Acts 2:38 Guide), **one primary content channel**, **one warm-outreach motion**, **one funnel.**
2. **Measure weekly** against the [scoreboard](11-scoreboard-kpis.md). Fix the weakest funnel step.
3. **Do not turn on paid ads** until the organic funnel is profitable.
4. **Every public asset** ends with "Get the free Acts 2:38 Guide."

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Days 1–14)

**Goal: the funnel exists end-to-end and can take money.**

- [ ] Perfect the **Acts 2:38 Guide** lead magnet (PDF + email series + at least 1 short video). Instant delivery, email-only opt-in.
- [ ] Build the **opt-in page** (seeker hook) and the **two sales pages** (Seeker Membership, Minister License) with the **offer stack + guarantees** visible ([03](03-grand-slam-offer.md)).
- [ ] Wire up **email/CRM**: welcome sequence, 7–10 email nurture, seeker→membership and member→minister ascension, dunning + win-back.
- [ ] Stand up **payments**: monthly + annual on both tiers; one-time for Kids; optional tripwire.
- [ ] Create the **minister sample pack** (10 sermon outlines / 5 lessons) — the minister lead magnet.
- [ ] Write the **3 core SOPs**: Study & Source, Doctrinal Review (the gate), Repurposing ([08](08-content-engine-faceless-ops.md)).
- [ ] Set up the **scoreboard** (a simple sheet/dashboard) with the weekly numbers.

## Phase 1 — Prove the funnel (Days 15–45)

**Goal: prove the funnel converts, and land the first ~25–50 ministers.**

- [ ] **Content channel live:** publish on the chosen primary channel (recommend short-form video answering salvation questions) — 3–5 assets/week from the "one study → ten assets" loop. Begin the SEO blog for evergreen pull.
- [ ] **Warm outreach push:** reach out personally to every Apostolic pastor/teacher in network. Offer the free sample pack first. Track replies → trials → licenses. Ask each yes for 2–3 referrals.
- [ ] **First conversions:** get the first paying Seekers and the first cohort of Ministers. Onboard them with the day-one win.
- [ ] **Instrument the funnel:** measure opt-in rate, nurture→membership %, sample→license %. Identify the weakest step.
- [ ] **Collect proof:** first testimonials (time saved, souls reached, kids who love the book).

**Phase 1 exit criteria:** funnel converts at a known, repeatable rate; ≥25 ministers or clear trajectory; weakest step identified.

## Phase 2 — Make it repeatable (Days 46–75)

**Goal: predictable weekly minister sign-ups + turn on cheap scale.**

- [ ] **Fix the weakest funnel step** found in Phase 1 (offer page, onboarding, nurture, or outreach script). Re-measure.
- [ ] **Turn on Referrals:** "refer a pastor, both get a free month"; church bulk-order referral perks. The networked niche makes this the cheapest growth.
- [ ] **Launch the Affiliate/Ambassador program:** recruit a handful of Apostolic bloggers/kids'-ministry leaders on recurring commission.
- [ ] **Kids front door live:** free coloring/verse pack → *The Day Everything Changed* bundle; begin parent-audience content.
- [ ] **Systematize outreach:** document the script that converts; consider a part-time outreach rep.
- [ ] **Push annual billing** on every checkout ("2 months free").

**Phase 2 exit criteria:** predictable weekly sign-ups from ≥2 sources (content + referral/outreach); annual-billing share rising; LTGP:CAC measured and > 3:1.

## Phase 3 — Pour fuel (Days 76–90)

**Goal: scale the proven channel and open the top of the ladder.**

- [ ] **Turn on paid ads** — *only now, only because the funnel is proven.* Cold → free guide; retarget → offers. Scale while LTGP:CAC > 3:1.
- [ ] **Launch the Church / Multi-Seat License** ($97/mo · $997/yr) and invite active license-holders with teams to upgrade.
- [ ] **Plan Kids title #2** (ages 6–8 series) into the quarterly pipeline.
- [ ] **Reinvest** profit into the constraint (content engine + outreach capacity).
- [ ] **90-day review:** against the scoreboard, decide the single constraint for the *next* 90 days and repeat the cycle.

## Beyond 90 days — the growth phases to $1M+

> Hormozi's "stages of scale": the constraint moves as you grow. Find it, fix it, repeat.

### Stage A — $0 → first $10k/mo: **Constraint = leads & proof**
Content + warm outreach + referrals. Get the funnel undeniably profitable. Collect proof. Push annual.

### Stage B — $10k → $50k/mo: **Constraint = consistent acquisition**
Add paid ads (funnel proven), scale affiliates, hire an outreach rep and content editor to the SOPs. Launch Church/Multi-seat. Kids catalog grows quarterly.

### Stage C — $50k → $100k+/mo: **Constraint = delivery & retention at scale**
Protect the doctrinal gate as the team grows. Harden onboarding, dunning, and community to crush churn. Institutional deals (Bible colleges, districts, church networks) for the Church License. Multiple Kids series shipping.

### Stage D — institutional scale: **Constraint = brand & infrastructure**
Kingdom Scribes becomes the *default* Apostolic resource library. Licensing deepens into networks/fellowships. The faceless, Word-centered brand and the doctrinal moat make it durable and acquirable.
