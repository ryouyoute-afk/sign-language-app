# 05 — Kingdom Scribes Kids (the children's division)

> A separate site, a separate avatar, a separate channel — but the same doctrine and the same funnel logic. Kids is both a **standalone product line** and a **second front door** to the whole ecosystem.

## Why Kids is strategically valuable (not just "nice")

1. **Lower-competition acquisition channel.** Apostolic *adults* content competes in a crowded faith space; Apostolic *kids* books that actually teach Acts 2:38 are nearly nonexistent. Blue ocean.
2. **Emotional, repeat-purchase product.** Parents who love one book buy the next, the bundle, the series. Higher purchase frequency than a single membership decision.
3. **Family flywheel.** Kids product → parent enters ecosystem → Seeker Membership → maybe Minister License (if a teacher/leader). And Sunday-school teachers (Avatar 2) buy classroom packs that tie back to the Minister License.
4. **Mission alignment.** Planting Acts 2:38 in children early is the purest expression of the mission — this division is where marketing and ministry are most clearly the same thing.

## The product line

### Flagship (live): *The Day Everything Changed — "A Lydia Story"*
- **Ages 3–5**, built around **Acts 2:38**.
- Full-color illustrated pages, KJV scripture, activity pages, and a prayer.
- **Don't sell it as a single book — sell it as a bundle** (see offer stacking below).

### Planned series (the roadmap to a real catalog)
- **Ages 6–8 series** — early readers; more narrative + comprehension; introduces repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, the Holy Ghost as a story arc.
- **Ages 9–12 series** — chapter books / devotional-style; deeper doctrine, "be a young scribe" identity, journaling components.
- **Family/Sunday-school tie-ins** — classroom packs, printable activities, memory-verse (KJV) cards, coloring sheets.

### Product ladder for Kids (mirrors the main value ladder)

## The Kids Grand Slam Offer (Value Equation)

- **Dream outcome ▲:** *"My child begs to re-read it AND is learning what our family actually believes."* (Two dreams: a book that *works* as entertainment + as discipleship.)
- **Likelihood ▲:** Professional illustration quality, age-graded reading level, activity pages that reinforce, a prayer that lets the parent close the loop. Sample pages and parent reviews de-risk the buy.
- **Time delay ▼:** Digital delivered instantly; print ships fast; read-aloud audio means a tired parent can press play *tonight*.
- **Effort ▼:** Parent discussion guide does the "how do I teach this?" work for them. Zero prep.

**Bundle stack (the hero $24.99 offer):**
| Item | Pain removed | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Print book | "I want something real on the shelf" | $14.99 |
| Activity pack (printables) | "Keep them engaged after" | $9.99 |
| Read-aloud audio | "I'm exhausted at bedtime" | $7.99 |
| Parent discussion guide | "How do I explain Acts 2:38 to a 4-yr-old?" | $9.99 |
| **Bonus:** memory-verse (KJV) cards | "Help it stick" | $4.99 |

Stacked ~$47 → **$24.99.** The gap is the offer.

## Kids-specific lead generation

The Core Four ([06](06-lead-generation.md)) applied to the parent avatar (Sister Grace):

- **Post free content:** Short read-aloud clips, illustration reveals, "teaching toddlers Acts 2:38" tips → on the platforms Apostolic moms use (Facebook groups, Instagram/Reels, Pinterest for printables, YouTube read-alouds).
- **Warm outreach:** Apostolic Sunday-school departments, kids'-ministry leaders, homeschool co-ops, church bookstores → sample + classroom-pack offer.
- **Free lead magnet:** Printable coloring page + memory-verse card (captures the parent's email → nurture → bundle).
- **Referral:** "Gift a copy to your church's nursery" / mom-to-mom sharing; church bulk-order discounts.
- **Affiliate/ambassador:** Apostolic mom-bloggers and kids'-ministry leaders earn on referrals.

## Cross-division mechanics

- **Membership members** get a Kids discount (cross-sell down-ladder buyers).
- **Minister License** includes/discounts the **Sunday-school classroom packs** (kids content as a license benefit → makes the license stickier *and* seeds kids-product demand among families).
- **Kids buyers** get invited into the Seeker Membership ("grow alongside your kids") and onto the email list.

## Kids division operating notes

- **Production pipeline:** writer (doctrine-checked) → illustrator → activity designer → narrator (audio) → doctrinal review → publish (print-on-demand + digital). Build this as a repeatable SOP so a new title ships each quarter ([08](08-content-engine-faceless-ops.md)).
- **Print fulfillment:** print-on-demand to avoid inventory risk early; move to bulk runs once a title proves out (better margin for church bulk orders).
- **Keep it faceless:** the "author" is Kingdom Scribes Kids; characters (Lydia, etc.) carry the brand, not a personality.
