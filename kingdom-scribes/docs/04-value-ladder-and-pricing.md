# 04 — Value Ladder & Pricing

> Hormozi: get a customer in the door with an irresistible front-end, then ascend them to higher-value offers. Maximize **LTGP** (lifetime gross profit), not first-sale revenue.

## The value ladder

```
                                          $$$$  CHURCH / MULTI-SEAT LICENSE (new tier)
                                                Whole-church access, district/college deals
                                       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                                 $$$   │ MINISTER LICENSE  $47/mo · $397/yr           │  ◄── PROFIT ENGINE
                                       │ Editable, brandable content for ministers    │
                              ┌────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
                        $$    │ SEEKER MEMBERSHIP  $12/mo · $97/yr                      │
                              │ Devotionals, studies, growth tracks                     │
                     ┌────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
                $    │ KIDS PRODUCTS  ~$15–$45 (books, bundles, series, subscription)     │
                     ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   FREE  ───────────┤ THE ACTS 2:38 GUIDE (5-part)  +  free studies / short-form content │  ◄── FRONT DOOR
                     └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The ascension paths:**
- **Seeker path:** Free Guide → Seeker Membership → (some) → become a worker/teacher → Minister License.
- **Minister path:** Free Guide or direct outreach → Minister License → Church/Multi-seat License.
- **Family path:** Free Guide / Kids freebie → Kids book → Kids bundle/series subscription → Seeker Membership (the parents).
- **Cross-sells everywhere:** Members get Kids discounts; ministers get kids-teaching templates; kids buyers get invited into membership.

## Pricing — keep, anchor, and extend

### Keep the current prices (they're well-structured)
| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Annual discount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seeker Membership | $12 | $97 | ~33% (3.3 mo free) |
| Minister License | $47 | $397 | ~30% (3.5 mo free) |

The **annual** options are the goal — they boost cash up front and crush churn. **Push annual hard** (it's the single biggest retention lever a subscription has).

### Add these tiers/products to extend the ladder

1. **Church / Multi-Seat License — $97/mo or $997/yr (NEW, top of ladder).**
   The pastor licenses for his *whole leadership team* (multiple teachers/seats). Higher ACV, stickier (whole church embeds it into operations), and a natural upsell from a single Minister License. This is where a 7-figure business is really built — institutional, not individual, buyers.

2. **Kids product pricing:**
   - Single illustrated book (print): **$14.99**; (digital): **$7.99**
   - Book + activity pack + read-aloud audio **bundle**: **$24.99**
   - Series subscription (new book each quarter): **$39/yr** or **$45 box**
   - Sunday-school classroom pack (printable, ties to Minister License): bundled into license or **$29** standalone

3. **One-time tripwires (optional, to monetize the free-guide traffic immediately):**
   A $9–$19 "New Convert Starter Pack" or "Acts 2:38 Deep-Dive" right after the free guide opt-in. Tripwires convert a *buyer* (people who have paid once buy again far more readily) and can self-liquidate ad spend.

### Pricing principles (Hormozi)
- **Price to value, not to cost.** Digital content has ~zero marginal cost; price on the *hours saved* and *risk removed* for a minister, not on "what a PDF is worth."
- **Don't compete on cheap.** A low price signals low value in a trust-based purchase. Hold prices; win on *guarantee + doctrinal trust + completeness*.
- **Raise prices as the library grows.** Founding-member lock-ins create urgency *and* let you raise rates for new entrants without churning loyalists.
- **Annual > monthly, always nudge up.** Offer "2 months free" framing on every monthly checkout.

## Retention = the real growth lever

Hormozi: *churn is the silent killer of recurring revenue.* For a content membership, retention strategy:

1. **Deliver a fast first win** (day-one usable resource / first devotional).
2. **Always be releasing** — the monthly "new and old" drop gives a reason to stay subscribed.
3. **Habit + identity** — growth tracks and "I'm a Kingdom Scribes minister" identity reduce cancellation.
4. **Annual billing** structurally removes 11 monthly churn decisions.
5. **Win-back & dunning** — failed-payment recovery sequence (a huge chunk of "churn" is just expired cards).
6. **Community pull** — the minister request line / community makes leaving feel like losing a relationship, not a file.

**Target:** Minister monthly churn < 4%; Seeker monthly churn < 7%. Every point of churn reduction is worth more than equivalent new acquisition.

| Tier | Monthly price | Assumed avg lifetime | Gross margin | **LTGP** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seeker | $12 (or $97/yr) | ~14 mo | ~92% | **~$155** |

| Church/Multi-seat | $97 (or $997/yr) | ~36 mo | ~92% | **~$3,200** |

> The Minister and Church tiers carry the business. Seeker + Kids feed the funnel and the mission. **Acquire on the front end; profit on ascension.**
