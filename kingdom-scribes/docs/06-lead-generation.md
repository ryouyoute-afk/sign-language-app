# 06 — Lead Generation: The Core Four

> Hormozi (*$100M Leads*): there are only four ways to get engaged leads. Do **one** until it's profitable and saturated, *then* add the next. A "lead" becomes an "engaged lead" when they give you contact info or respond.

## The lead magnet (the bait): the **Acts 2:38 Guide**

This already exists and is perfect. Hormozi's lead-magnet test: *reveal a problem, then give a real, complete solution to a narrow slice of it* — so good they'd have paid for it.

- **Problem it reveals:** "Most of what you've been told about being saved isn't what the New Testament church actually believed."
- **Complete solution to a slice:** the 5-part walk through repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the Holy Ghost — straight from Scripture.
- **Naturally leads to the paid offer:** once someone *believes* Acts 2:38, they want to *grow* (Seeker Membership) or *teach* it (Minister License).

**Optimize the magnet:** make it genuinely excellent, multi-format (PDF + email series + short videos), require only an email, and deliver instantly. Add a soft tripwire after opt-in ([04](04-value-ladder-and-pricing.md)).

> **Secondary magnets** (one per avatar, for channel-specific hooks): "10 Sermon Outlines on Acts 2:38" (Pastor Mark), "5 Ready Sunday-School Lessons" (Sister Ruth), "Lydia coloring + memory-verse pack" (Sister Grace).

## The Core Four — in priority order for Kingdom Scribes

### ① Post Free Content Publicly *(PRIMARY — fits faceless + builds the durable asset)*
**Why first:** It's free, it compounds, it's perfectly suited to a faceless, Word-centered brand, and it warms all four avatars at once.

- **Format:** short-form video (Reels/Shorts/TikTok) of Scripture-on-screen teaching + voiceover, faceless (text, KJV verses, illustration, narration). Plus long-form (YouTube studies, blog/SEO articles answering salvation questions).
- **The hooks:** answer the exact questions seekers type — *"Why be baptized in Jesus' name?"*, *"What is the Holy Ghost?"*, *"Is Acts 2:38 for today?"* These pull in Brother James organically.
- **SEO play:** Kingdom Scribes can *own* the search results for Apostolic salvation questions and "Apostolic sermon outlines / Sunday school lessons." Evergreen, compounding, free traffic → free guide → ladder.
- **Faceless content engine:** detailed in [08](08-content-engine-faceless-ops.md). One studied passage → a Reel, a short, a blog post, an email, a carousel, a sermon-outline sample. ("New and old," repurposed.)
- **CTA on everything:** "Get the free Acts 2:38 Guide."

### ② Warm Outreach *(PRIMARY for ministers — fastest path to revenue)*
**Why now:** The Apostolic world is densely networked. You can *directly* reach pastors and teachers. This is the fastest route to the high-LTV Minister License and doesn't wait on algorithm scale.

- **Who:** Apostolic pastors, Sunday-school directors, Bible-college contacts, district/fellowship networks, existing relationships.
- **The motion:** personal, non-spammy: *"We built a doctrinally-vetted, brandable resource library for Apostolic ministers — can I send you 10 free sermon outlines and a few kids' lessons to try this Sunday?"* Give first (free samples = the lead magnet), then invite to the license.
- **Leverage the network:** every pastor who says yes → ask for 2–3 other pastors who'd want the same. (Warm outreach + referral compounding.)
- **Channels:** email, text, calls, DMs, conferences/camp meetings/district events (in person is gold here).

### ③ Run Paid Ads *(LATER — only after the organic funnel is proven profitable)*
**Why wait:** Don't pour money into a funnel that doesn't yet convert. Once content + warm outreach prove the funnel (free guide → membership/license at a known rate), *then* paid ads buy scale.

- **Cold ads → free Acts 2:38 Guide** (never cold-sell the membership). Capture email, nurture, ascend.
- **Retarget** site visitors and guide-readers with the Seeker/Minister offers + guarantee.
- **Kids ads** to the parent/homeschool audience → free coloring pack → book bundle.
- **Watch one number:** CAC vs. LTGP. Scale only while **LTGP:CAC > 3:1** ([09](09-financial-model.md)).

### ④ Cold Outreach *(SELECTIVE)*
- Cold email/DM to Apostolic churches with a public contact + a genuine free-sample offer. Lower priority than warm, but the church directory is finite and targetable. Keep it value-first and respectful, never spammy (guardrails apply).

## Lead-getters: the four ways to *multiply* the Core Four

Hormozi: once *you* can get leads, get *others* to do it for you. Roll these out in Phase 2+.

| Lead-getter | Kingdom Scribes application |
|---|---|
| **Referrals** | "Refer a pastor, you both get a free month." Bulk church-order referral perks. Mom-to-mom kids sharing. The networked niche makes this enormously powerful. |
| **Affiliates / Ambassadors** | Apostolic bloggers, kids'-ministry leaders, evangelists earn recurring commission on memberships/licenses they refer. |
| **Employees / Contractors** | A small outreach person doing warm/cold minister outreach once the script is proven. Content-team scribes producing the engine. |
| **Agencies** | Outsource paid-ads management once spend justifies it — only after the funnel is proven. |

---

## The lead-gen sequencing rule (don't skip this)

1. **Now:** Free guide perfected → **Content (①)** + **Warm outreach (②)** running together. One funnel. Measure weekly.
2. **When the funnel is profitable:** add **Referrals** + **Affiliates** (cheapest scale).
3. **When you have profit to reinvest:** turn on **Paid ads (③)**, scale while LTGP:CAC > 3:1.
4. **When ads + referrals saturate:** add **Cold outreach (④)** and **employees/agencies**.

> **Do not** run all four channels half-heartedly on day one. **One channel, fully worked, until it pays.** That is the entire Hormozi lead discipline.
