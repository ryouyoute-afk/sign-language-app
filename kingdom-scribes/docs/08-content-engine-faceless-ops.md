# 08 — Content Engine & Faceless Operations

> The brand is the Word, not a face. That means the *system* of producing content is the real asset. This file is how Kingdom Scribes produces "things new and old" (Matthew 13:52) at scale without a personality and without burning out.

## The core production loop: "One Study → Ten Assets"

Every unit of work starts as **one deeply-studied passage or doctrine** (e.g., Acts 2:38, the Oneness, holiness, prayer & fasting). From that single study, the engine spins out:

```
            ┌──────────────► Short-form video (Reel/Short/TikTok) — Scripture-on-screen + voiceover
            │
            ├──────────────► YouTube long-form study
            │
            ├──────────────► SEO blog article (answers a search query)
            │
ONE STUDY ──┼──────────────► Email for the nurture sequence
            │
            ├──────────────► Carousel / image post
            │
            ├──────────────► Minister-License asset: editable sermon outline
            │
            ├──────────────► Seeker-Membership asset: devotional / study page
            │
            └──────────────► Kids tie-in: a lesson or activity (where age-appropriate)
```

**Why this is the cheat code:** the expensive part is the *study and doctrinal vetting* — done once. The cheap part is reformatting. You get free-content marketing **and** paid-product inventory from the same labor. Marketing and product are the same pipeline.

## The faceless content formats (what works without a face)

- Scripture text on screen with narration (AI or human voice — narration is not a "face").
- Illustrated/animated explainers (especially for Kids).
- KJV verse graphics + quote cards.
- Whiteboard/slide-style teaching.
- Audio: narrated studies, kids' read-alouds, devotional audio.
- Long-form written: blog/SEO, downloadable studies.

> Faceless ≠ impersonal. Warmth comes from the *voice, the truth, and the craft*, not a personality on camera.

## The production system (SOPs so it's repeatable, not heroic)

Build each of these as a written SOP + checklist so any contractor can run it:

1. **Study & Source SOP** — how a topic is researched, which references are allowed (KJV + sound Apostolic sources), what gets written.
2. **Doctrinal Review SOP** — *mandatory gate.* Nothing publishes without an Apostolic doctrinal check against the [guardrails](01-mission-and-doctrinal-guardrails.md). This is the brand's whole value — protect it ruthlessly.
3. **Repurposing SOP** — the "one study → ten assets" checklist with format specs.
4. **Publishing & Scheduling SOP** — where/when each asset goes, with the CTA to the free guide on every public asset.
5. **Kids Production SOP** — writer → illustrator → activity designer → narrator → doctrinal review → POD/digital publish.
6. **License-Asset Formatting SOP** — how content becomes *editable & brandable* (templated Word/Docs/Canva/PDF with placeholders for church name/logo).

## The team (lean, faceless, doctrine-gated)

You don't need many people — you need the *right roles* with the doctrinal gate non-negotiable.

| Role | Responsibility | When to add |
|---|---|---|
| **Doctrinal reviewer** (can be the founder/pastor) | The gate. Approves everything. | Day one — never skip |
| **Content scribe/writer** | Produces studies & repurposes into assets | Day one (or founder) |
| **Short-form editor** | Cuts Reels/Shorts from studies | When content is the proven channel |
| **Illustrator (Kids)** | Books, activity pages | When shipping the next Kids title |
| **Narrator / audio** | Read-alouds, devotional audio | As audio demand grows |
| **Outreach rep** | Warm/cold minister outreach (proven script) | Phase 2, once the script converts |
| **Ops/VA** | Scheduling, fulfillment, support, dunning | When volume justifies |

**Hiring principle:** systematize first, hire to the system. A documented SOP turns a $X/hr contractor into reliable output without diluting doctrine.

## The tech stack (keep it boring and integrated)

- **Site/membership/licensing:** a platform that handles gated content + subscriptions + license delivery (e.g., a membership platform or LMS).
- **Email/CRM:** segmented by avatar; runs nurture, ascension, dunning, win-back.
- **Payments:** subscriptions (monthly + annual), one-time (kids/tripwires), failed-payment recovery.
- **Kids fulfillment:** print-on-demand + digital delivery; bulk runs once titles prove out.
- **Analytics:** funnel-step conversion tracking + the [scoreboard](11-scoreboard-kpis.md).
- **Content ops:** a simple editorial calendar + asset library (so the "one study → ten assets" pipeline is visible and repeatable).

> Don't over-engineer the stack. Pick tools that integrate, ship, and let you measure the funnel. The *content system* and the *doctrinal gate* are the moat — not the software.

## The cadence (what "the machine" runs at)

- **Weekly:** 1 deep study → repurposed into the week's public content (3–5 short-form + 1 long-form + 1 blog/email) + at least 1 new license asset + 1 membership asset.
- **Monthly:** the "new and old" drop for members and ministers (the retention engine).
- **Quarterly:** a new Kids title or major series asset.
- **Always:** every public asset ends with "Get the free Acts 2:38 Guide."

This cadence is the heartbeat. Protect it, measure it, and only scale a channel after it's proven.
