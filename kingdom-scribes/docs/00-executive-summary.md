# 00 — Executive Summary

## The business in one paragraph

Kingdom Scribes is a **faceless, KJV-only Apostolic content library and licensing platform**. It earns trust by giving away the clearest Acts 2:38 salvation guide on the internet, monetizes seekers through a $12/mo membership, and earns the bulk of its profit by **licensing editable, brandable content to ministers** ($47/mo) who then distribute the Word to entire congregations. A children's division (*Kingdom Scribes Kids*) extends the doctrine to families through illustrated KJV books, opening a second, lower-competition acquisition channel and a recurring family-product line.

## Why this works (the Hormozi thesis)

1. **A starving crowd already exists.** Apostolic Pentecostals are an underserved niche actively hunting for doctrinally pure, ready-to-use resources. Most "Christian content" online is theologically generic or contradicts their doctrine. We are not competing on *more* content — we are the *only* content that fits. (See [02 — Market & Avatars](02-market-and-avatars.md).)
2. **The offer is a Grand Slam Offer, not a product.** We don't sell "devotionals." We sell a minister *hours of his week back* and the *confidence that every word he hands his flock is doctrinally sound* — with a guarantee. (See [03 — Grand Slam Offer](03-grand-slam-offer.md).)
3. **The Minister License is a force multiplier.** One licensed minister is not one customer — he is a **distribution node** who puts the content in front of 50–500 people weekly. This is the cheat code: our best customers do our evangelism *and* our marketing. (See [04 — Value Ladder](04-value-ladder-and-pricing.md).)
4. **Faceless = scalable and durable.** No personality risk, no creator burnout, no platform-dependence on one face. The brand is the Word. This is a *moat*, not a limitation. (See [08 — Content Engine](08-content-engine-faceless-ops.md).)

## The money math (target unit economics)

| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Free guide → Seeker conversion | 4–8% | Front-door efficiency |
| Seeker → Minister upgrade | 8–15% | The real profit lever |
| Minister License LTGP | ~$1,400+ | (See [09](09-financial-model.md) for derivation) |
| Blended CAC (organic-led) | < $90 | Mostly content + referral |
| **LTGP : CAC** | **> 3:1 (target 5:1+)** | Hormozi's go/no-go line |

If LTGP:CAC holds above 3:1, **every dollar of profit reinvested into the Core Four buys more growth than it costs.** That is the entire engine.

## The single constraint to attack first

Hormozi's rule: **find the one constraint throttling the business and pour everything into it.** Today that constraint is **not** product (the content exists/can be produced) and **not** monetization (prices are set). It is **lead flow** — not enough qualified seekers and ministers know this exists.

> **First 90 days = build the lead machine.** One free offer (Acts 2:38 Guide), one primary channel (post free content / short-form video + a warm-outreach push to Apostolic pastors), one funnel, measured weekly. Don't add a second channel until the first is profitable. (See [10 — 90-Day Roadmap](10-90-day-roadmap.md).)

## The 12-month picture (base case)

- **Months 1–3:** Lead magnet + funnel live, content engine producing 3–5 assets/week, first 50 ministers via direct outreach. *Goal: prove the funnel converts.*
- **Months 4–6:** Turn on referrals + affiliate/minister-ambassador program. Launch paid ads only after organic funnel is profitable. *Goal: predictable weekly minister sign-ups.*
- **Months 7–12:** Scale the winning channel, launch Kids Series #2, add the church/multi-seat license tier. *Goal: cross $1M annual run-rate on combined memberships + licenses + kids products.* (See [09](09-financial-model.md) for the three scenarios.)

## What this plan deliberately does NOT do

- It does not chase every social platform at once. **One channel until it pays.**
- It does not build a personality brand. **Faceless is the strategy.**
- It does not water down doctrine to widen the market. **The niche IS the moat.** (See [12 — Risks & Stewardship](12-risks-and-stewardship.md).)
