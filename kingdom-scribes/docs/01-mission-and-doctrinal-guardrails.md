# 01 — Mission & Doctrinal Guardrails

> Every tactic in this plan must pass through this file first. If a growth idea violates a guardrail, the guardrail wins. Always.

## The mission

To put doctrinally sound, KJV-grounded, Apostolic resources into the hands of every seeker, saint, minister, teacher, and child who wants them — removing every obstacle between a person and the truth of Acts 2:38, and between a minister and the tools to preach it faithfully.

We are a **scribe instructed unto the kingdom** (Matthew 13:52): bringing forth treasure both **new** (fresh studies, modern formats, illustrated kids' books, editable templates) and **old** (the unchanging Word, the apostles' doctrine, the King James Bible).

## The non-negotiable doctrinal position

These define what we will and will not publish. They are also our **positioning moat** — the reason a starving niche chooses us over generic Christian content.

1. **The King James Bible** is our text. KJV-only.
2. **Acts 2:38 salvation** — repentance, water baptism in the name of Jesus Christ, and the infilling of the Holy Ghost.
3. **The Oneness of God** — Jesus is the name in which Father, Son, and Holy Ghost is revealed.
4. **Holy living / holiness** — practical sanctification, separation from the world.
5. **The apostles' doctrine** as the standard (Acts 2:42), not denominational consensus or cultural trend.

Everything we sell is explicitly **Apostolic** — not "general Christian." That specificity is a feature, not a constraint.

## The "faceless by design" principle

Kingdom Scribes is built around **the Word, the doctrine, and trusted content** — not a personality. This is a strategic asset:

- **No personality risk.** A brand built on one person can be destroyed by that person. A brand built on the Word cannot.
- **Trust transfers to the doctrine, not a guru.** Buyers trust *that it's sound*, which is exactly what a minister needs before he hands it to his flock.
- **Infinitely scalable team.** Writers, illustrators, and editors can be added without "diluting the founder's voice," because the voice is Scripture.
- **It models the right posture.** The messenger decreases; the message increases.

**Guardrail:** No tactic may require a "founder face," personal-brand influencer play, or cult-of-personality marketing. Testimonies and fruit are fine; idolizing a person is not.

## The marketing-vs-ministry line (read this twice)

Hormozi's frameworks optimize for *value delivered* and *attention not wasted*. Used rightly, that is good stewardship. Used wrongly, it becomes manipulation. The line:

| Permitted (stewardship) | Forbidden (manipulation) |
|---|---|
| Real urgency (a series cohort genuinely starts Monday) | Fake countdown timers / invented scarcity |
| Honest testimonies of changed lives | Fabricated or exaggerated results |
| Clear, bold claims about doctrine | Bait-and-switch, hiding the Apostolic position to widen reach |
| Guarantees that remove risk | Guarantees we won't honor |
| Pricing that funds the mission | Squeezing seekers who genuinely can't pay (see hardship policy) |

**Hardship / benevolence policy (build it in from day one):** No seeker is turned away from *salvation content* for lack of money. The free Acts 2:38 Guide is always free. A scholarship/benevolence path exists for the Seeker Membership. We monetize **ministers and convenience**, not access to the gospel.

## The brand voice

- **Bold, doctrinal, uncompromising** — but not arrogant or contentious.
- **Plain KJV-rooted language.** Scripture is quoted, not paraphrased into mush.
- **Confident, not defensive.** We state what we believe; we don't spend our energy attacking other groups by name.
- **"Things new and old."** Modern formats, ancient truth.

## How to use this file

Before launching any campaign, offer, or piece of content, ask:
1. Does it hold the doctrinal line without compromise?
2. Does it keep the brand faceless and Word-centered?
3. Is it on the stewardship side of the marketing line?
4. Would a sound Apostolic pastor be proud to hand it to his congregation?

If all four are "yes," proceed. If any is "no," fix it or kill it.
