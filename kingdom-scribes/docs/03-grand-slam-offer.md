# 03 — The Grand Slam Offer

> Hormozi: *"A Grand Slam Offer is an offer so good people feel stupid saying no."* You build it with the **Value Equation**, then stack, name, guarantee, and add scarcity/urgency.

## The Value Equation

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Dream Outcome} \times \text{Perceived Likelihood of Achievement}}{\text{Time Delay} \times \text{Effort \& Sacrifice}}$$

To increase value: **raise** the top two, **shrink** the bottom two. Here's how each Kingdom Scribes offer does it.

---

## OFFER A — The Minister License (the profit engine)

**Current:** $47/mo or $397/yr — editable content ministers can brand and distribute.

This is the offer that matters most. Let's make it a Grand Slam.

### Value Equation applied
- **Dream outcome ▲** — Not "content." The dream is: *"Every Sunday I step up fully prepared, 100% confident every word is doctrinally sound, with my church's name on it — and I got my week back."*
- **Perceived likelihood ▲** — Vetted-Apostolic guarantee, samples they can hold before buying, testimonials from real pastors, a doctrinal statement they can verify line-by-line. Risk reversal (below) makes the "yes" feel safe.
- **Time delay ▼** — Instant access. Ready-to-use *this* Sunday. Don't make them wait for value — give a usable resource in the first 5 minutes.
- **Effort & sacrifice ▼** — Editable templates (Word/Google/Canva/PDF), pre-formatted, branded in two clicks. No design skill, no theology degree, no building from scratch.

### The offer stack (what's *included* — make the price look trivial next to the pile)

| Component | The pain it removes | Standalone value |
|---|---|---|
| **Core editable library** — sermon outlines, Bible studies, devotionals, evangelism tools | "Building from scratch at midnight" | $1,200/yr |
| **Sunday-school & kids teaching templates** (ties to Kids division) | "Nothing sound for the children" | $600/yr |
| **Brand-it-as-your-own license** — put the church's name/logo on it | "Looks generic / not ours" | $400/yr |
| **New-drop cadence** — fresh "new and old" content every month | "I'll run out of material" | $480/yr |
| **Doctrinal-soundness guarantee** — every asset Apostolic-vetted | "I can't risk teaching error" | priceless |
| **Minister community / request line** — request topics, get them produced | "I need something specific" | $300/yr |
| **Quick-start pack** — 10 ready sermons + 10 lessons on day one | "I need something *this week*" | $250 |
| **Bonus: Evangelism kit** — Acts 2:38 tracts/series, brandable | "I want to win souls, not just preach" | $200 |

**Stacked value:** ~$3,400+/yr. **Price:** $397/yr. The gap is the offer.

### Naming (Hormozi's M-A-G-I-C, lightly applied)
- *Magnetic reason:* why now — congregations need feeding every week.
- *Avatar:* "for the Apostolic pastor & teacher."
- *Goal:* "preach fully prepared."
- *Interval/container:* "License."
- **Working names:** "The Apostolic Pulpit License," "The Scribe's License," "The Minister's Treasury (new & old)." Keep it doctrinal, not gimmicky.

## OFFER B — The Seeker Membership (the mission funnel)

**Current:** $12/mo or $97/yr — personal growth.

### Value Equation applied
- **Dream ▲:** *"Grow daily in Apostolic truth and actually live it — not just read, but become."*
- **Likelihood ▲:** Structured plans (30-day "Receiving the Holy Ghost," "Living Holy," "New Convert Foundations"), so it's not a pile of PDFs but a *path*.

### Offer stack
- Full devotional + study library (members area)
- Guided growth tracks (New Convert, Holy Ghost, Holiness, Prayer & Fasting)
- Searchable Scripture-topic index (KJV)
- Printable studies for personal/family worship

**Frame the price:** $97/yr ≈ **27¢/day** to grow in the Word. Anchor against the value of one Christian book (~$15) or one conference ticket (~$150+).

## Risk reversal (guarantees) — the same across offers

Hormozi: a bold guarantee is the single biggest conversion lever. Ours, tuned to the mission:

1. **The Doctrinal Soundness Guarantee (unconditional):** *"Every resource is written from a KJV-only Apostolic position. If you ever find a line that contradicts the apostles' doctrine, tell us — we'll fix it and credit your account."* (Builds the core trust ministers need.)
2. **The "Ready This Sunday" Guarantee (Minister License):** *"If you can't pull a usable, brandable resource within your first 10 minutes, we'll refund your first month and help you personally."*
3. **30-Day Money-Back (Seeker & Kids):** *"Grow for 30 days. If it doesn't feed your soul, full refund — keep the free guide."*

> These are honored, not theater (see [01 — Guardrails](01-mission-and-doctrinal-guardrails.md)).

## Scarcity & urgency — only the *honest* kind

- **Real cohorts:** Guided seeker tracks and minister "new-drop" cadence start on real dates → "next cohort begins Monday" is true.
- **Founding-member pricing:** Genuinely lock early adopters at a lower rate *that will actually rise* as the library grows. Only use if you'll honor it.
- **Limited live elements:** If a monthly live Q&A/training for ministers has a real cap, say so.
- **Never:** fake timers, invented "only 3 left" on digital goods, manipulative countdowns.

## The "feel stupid saying no" test

For Pastor Mark: *"For about a dollar a day, I get a constantly-growing library of vetted Apostolic sermons, studies, and kids' lessons I can brand as my own and use this Sunday — guaranteed sound or it's fixed and credited — and it saves me 5+ hours a week."*

That's the bar. Every offer page must make the value pile so tall the price looks like a rounding error.
