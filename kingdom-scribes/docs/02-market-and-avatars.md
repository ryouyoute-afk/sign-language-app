# 02 — Market & Avatars

> Hormozi: *"You don't need a bigger market. You need a starving crowd."* And: *"The riches are in the niches."*

## The starving crowd

The Apostolic / Oneness Pentecostal world is a **narrow, deep, underserved, and highly-networked** market:

- **Underserved by content tools.** The vast majority of online Christian resources (devotional apps, sermon libraries, Sunday-school curricula, kids' Bibles) are either doctrinally generic or actively contradict Apostolic doctrine (Trinitarian language, sprinkling, "sinner's prayer" salvation, modern translations). An Apostolic minister cannot safely grab a random resource off the shelf — he has to vet every line.
- **High switching pain, high loyalty.** Once a minister finds a source he *trusts* doctrinally, he stays and tells his network.
- **Densely networked.** Apostolic fellowships, conferences, district meetings, Bible colleges, and online groups mean word travels fast. One delighted pastor → ten referrals.
- **Recurring need.** Every week there's a new sermon, a new lesson, a new convert to disciple, a new child in Sunday school. The content need never ends → recurring revenue is natural.

**Why "niche" is the moat, not the ceiling:** A broader "Christian content" brand would have more total addressable buyers but **zero differentiation and constant doctrinal compromise**. By being unmistakably Apostolic, Kingdom Scribes becomes the *default* for a specific tribe — and defaults win.

## Market sizing logic (directional, not precise)

We don't need exact numbers to act; we need order-of-magnitude and a path. The relevant pools:

- **Apostolic/Oneness churches** across the US (and globally) → each has 1 pastor + multiple teachers/leaders = the **Minister License** pool.
- **Members & seekers** inside and adjacent to those churches → the **Seeker Membership** + **Kids** pool.
- **Online seekers** searching salvation questions ("baptism in Jesus name," "what is the Holy Ghost," "Acts 2:38") → top-of-funnel for the free guide.

Even a *single-digit-percent* penetration of the Minister pool, at $397/yr, plus the membership and kids lines, clears seven figures. (Math in [09 — Financial Model](09-financial-model.md).) The constraint is **awareness**, not market size.

## The four avatars

Hormozi: speak to **one specific person** at a time. We have four, in priority order of *profit contribution*.

### Avatar 1 — "Pastor Mark," the bivocational Apostolic pastor *(PRIMARY — highest LTV)*
- **Who:** Pastors a 40–120 member Apostolic church. Often works a day job. Preps sermons late at night. Wife runs the kids' ministry.
- **The pain (his words):** *"I'm preaching three times a week, running the church, and working full-time. I don't have hours to build a Sunday-school curriculum from scratch, and I won't use anything that isn't sound."*
- **Dream outcome:** Show up Sunday fully prepared, with material he's 100% confident is doctrinally pure, that he can put his own church's name on.
- **What he buys:** **Minister License** ($47/mo, $397/yr). Editable, brandable studies, sermon outlines, kids' lessons, evangelism tools.
- **Why he's #1:** Highest price, highest retention, and he is a **distribution node** — he multiplies the content to his whole flock and refers other pastors.

### Avatar 2 — "Sister Ruth," the Sunday-school teacher / kids' ministry leader
- **Who:** Volunteer, deeply committed, juggling 8–20 kids across ages with no budget and no time.
- **The pain:** *"I need lessons and activities that are fun for the kids but actually teach Acts 2:38 — not generic 'Jesus loves you' material."*
- **Dream outcome:** Walk into class with a ready-to-go, illustrated, age-appropriate, doctrinally sound lesson + activity.
- **What she buys:** **Kingdom Scribes Kids** products + (often via her pastor's) Minister License for the teaching templates.
- **Why she matters:** She's the bridge between the adult library and the kids' division, and a passionate word-of-mouth engine.

### Avatar 3 — "Brother James," the seeker / new convert
- **Who:** Recently encountered Apostolic truth (online, or invited to church). Hungry, full of questions, maybe coming out of a Trinitarian or unchurched background.
- **The pain:** *"I want to understand what this church believes about being saved — and I want it straight from the Bible, not someone's opinion."*
- **Dream outcome:** Clear, Scripture-backed understanding of salvation; a path to grow daily.
- **What he buys:** Enters free via the **Acts 2:38 Guide** → converts to **Seeker Membership** ($12/mo).
- **Why he matters:** Volume top-of-funnel, the *mission* heartbeat, and tomorrow's saints (and some of tomorrow's ministers).

### Avatar 4 — "Sister Grace," the Apostolic mom *(KIDS buyer)*
- **Who:** Wants her 3–8 year olds reading books that reinforce what the family believes, not Trinitarian or worldly kids' content.
- **The pain:** *"Every cute Christian kids' book I find teaches something we don't believe, or nothing at all. I want my kids to fall in love with Acts 2:38."*
- **Dream outcome:** Beautiful, fun books her kids beg to re-read that plant Apostolic truth early.
- **What she buys:** **Kingdom Scribes Kids** books/series (*The Day Everything Changed*, future 6–8 and 9–12 series), bundles, subscriptions.
- **Why she matters:** Opens a **lower-competition acquisition channel** (parenting/homeschool/faith-mom spaces) and a repeat-purchase product line.

## Avatar priority & why

| Priority | Avatar | Primary product | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pastor Mark | Minister License | Highest LTV + distribution multiplier + referrals |
| 2 | Sister Ruth | Kids + license templates | Bridges divisions, evangelizes for us |
| 3 | Brother James | Seeker Membership | Volume funnel, mission core, future ministers |
| 4 | Sister Grace | Kids products | New channel, repeat purchases |

**Strategic implication:** Spend the *first* marketing dollars and outreach hours on **Pastor Mark**, because landing one pastor cascades into seekers (his converts), a kids buyer (his families), and referrals (his network). He is the keystone.
