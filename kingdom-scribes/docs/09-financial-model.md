# 09 — Financial Model

> Hormozi's master ratio: **LTGP : CAC**. If lifetime gross profit per customer is at least 3× what it costs to acquire them, you have a business you can scale by spending. Below 3:1, fix the offer/funnel before you spend a dollar on ads.

> ⚠️ These are **illustrative planning numbers** to pressure-test the model and set targets — not a forecast. Replace each assumption with your real data as it comes in. The *structure* is the point.

## Unit economics by tier

| Tier | Price | Avg lifetime | Gross margin | **LTGP** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seeker Membership | $12/mo (~$97/yr) | ~14 mo | ~92% | **~$155** |
| Minister License | $47/mo (~$397/yr) | ~30 mo | ~92% | **~$1,400** |

*Digital content margins are very high (~90%+); print/POD kids products are lower (~50–60%). Blended business margin lands high because licenses/memberships dominate gross profit.*

## CAC targets by channel

| Channel | Expected CAC | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Organic content (SEO/short-form) | **~$0–30** | Time/labor cost; compounds for free over time |
| Warm outreach (ministers) | **~$20–60** | Mostly labor; very high conversion in a networked niche |
| Referral / affiliate | **~$30–80** | Pay-on-performance; cheap scale |
| Paid ads (cold → free guide) | **~$60–150** blended to a paying customer | Only after funnel proven |

**Blended target CAC (organic-led early): < $90.**

## The ratio check

| Tier | LTGP | CAC (blended) | **LTGP:CAC** | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minister License | $1,400 | $90 | **~15:1** | Scale aggressively |
| Church/Multi-seat | $3,200 | $120 | **~27:1** | Scale aggressively |
| Seeker Membership | $155 | $50 | **~3:1** | Healthy; feeds the funnel |
| Kids | $34 | $25 | **~1.4:1** | Acquisition/mission play; profit via cross-sell to membership |

> **The takeaway:** Ministers and churches are wildly profitable to acquire — pour resources there. Seekers roughly self-fund. Kids may run near break-even *on first product alone* but win through the **family flywheel** (parent → membership) and mission value. Judge Kids on *blended* LTV including the membership it feeds, not the first book.

## Where the money goes (reinvestment priority)

Hormozi: reinvest profit into the **constraint**. Early, the constraint is leads → reinvest into the proven acquisition channel.

1. **Content engine** (the durable, compounding asset) — fund this first.
2. **Warm-outreach capacity** (an outreach rep once the script converts) — fastest minister growth.
3. **Referral/affiliate payouts** (pay-on-performance — only costs when it works).
4. **Paid ads** — *only* after the funnel is proven, scale while LTGP:CAC > 3:1.
5. **Kids catalog expansion** (new titles = new acquisition + repeat revenue).
6. **Ops/automation** (dunning, onboarding, support) to protect retention.

## Cash-flow notes (the make-or-break details)

- **Push annual billing** → cash up front funds growth and slashes churn. This single behavior can be the difference between starving and self-funding.
- **Failed-payment recovery (dunning)** → recovers 20–40% of "churn" that's just expired cards. Cheapest revenue you'll ever get.
- **POD for Kids** → no inventory cash tied up early; move to bulk runs only once a title proves out (and for church bulk orders).
- **Benevolence carve-out** → budget a small % for hardship scholarships ([01 guardrail](01-mission-and-doctrinal-guardrails.md)); it's both right and good word-of-mouth.

## The one financial rule to run by

> Keep **LTGP:CAC above 3:1** and **push annual billing**. As long as both hold, every dollar of profit reinvested into the proven channel buys more than a dollar of future profit — and the business compounds.
