# 11 — Scoreboard & KPIs

> Hormozi: *"What gets measured gets managed."* Run the business off a small set of numbers reviewed on a fixed cadence. Don't track 50 metrics — track the few that move the constraint, and fix the **weakest** one.

## The one-screen weekly scoreboard

Review every **Monday**. If a number is red, that's the week's focus.

| # | Metric | Definition | Early target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New engaged leads** | Free-guide opt-ins + sample-pack downloads this week | Growing W/W | Top of funnel = the current constraint |
| 2 | **Opt-in rate** | Opt-ins ÷ opt-in page visitors | 25–40% | Page/offer efficiency |
| 3 | **Lead → Seeker %** | Seeker conversions ÷ leads | 4–8% | Seeker funnel health |
| 4 | **Sample → Minister %** | Minister conversions ÷ sample downloads | 10–20% | The profit funnel |
| 5 | **New Ministers** | Net new Minister Licenses | Growing W/W | The profit engine |
| 6 | **New MRR** | New recurring revenue added | Growing W/W | Real growth |
| 7 | **Churn %** (each tier) | Cancels ÷ active | Minister <4%, Seeker <7% | The silent killer |
| 8 | **Annual mix %** | Annual subs ÷ total subs | Rising | Cash + retention lever |
| 9 | **CAC** (blended) | Spend (incl. labor) ÷ new customers | < $90 | Half the master ratio |
| 10 | **LTGP:CAC** | From [09](09-financial-model.md) | > 3:1 (aim 5:1+) | The go/no-go for scaling spend |

## Monthly review metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **MRR & ARR** | Total recurring revenue | Up M/M |
| **LTGP per tier** | Realized lifetime gross profit | Holding/rising |
| **Net revenue retention** | Expansion (upgrades) − churn | > 100% (upgrades outrun churn) |
| **Minister → Church upgrade %** | Multi-seat conversions | 10–15% over time |
| **Kids: repeat-purchase rate** | Buyers who buy again | Rising with catalog |

| **Content output** | Studies → assets shipped | On cadence ([08](08-content-engine-faceless-ops.md)) |
| **Organic traffic / SEO rank** | Compounding free traffic | Up M/M |
| **Dunning recovery %** | Failed payments recovered | 20–40% |

## Mission metrics (this is a ministry — measure the fruit)

Hold these next to the money. They are the *why*.

| Metric | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| **Free guides delivered** | Gospel reach (Acts 2:38 in front of seekers) |
| **Testimonies / changed lives** | Repentance, baptisms, Holy Ghost reports |
| **Churches served** | Distribution reach via ministers |
| **Children reached** | Kids books in homes/classrooms |
| **Benevolence/scholarships granted** | No one turned away from the gospel |

> If money metrics rise while mission metrics flatline, something has drifted from the [guardrails](01-mission-and-doctrinal-guardrails.md). Both must move together.

## How to actually use the scoreboard

1. **Find the weakest step**, not the lowest number — a low metric that's *cheap to double* beats a high one that's maxed.
2. **One focus per week.** Pick the red metric, run one improvement, re-measure next Monday.
3. **Compare to last week + the target**, not to vanity peaks.
4. **Labor counts as cost.** Faceless content isn't "free" — load CAC with real time/cost so LTGP:CAC is honest.
5. **Protect the gate.** Content *output* is a metric; content that bypassed doctrinal review is a defect, not output.

## The two numbers that decide everything

If you could only watch two:

1. **LTGP:CAC** — are we acquiring customers profitably? (If yes, spend more. If no, fix the funnel.)
2. **Net new Minister/Church MRR** — is the profit engine growing? (Everything else feeds this.)

Keep both green and the business compounds. Let either go red and that's the next constraint to attack.
