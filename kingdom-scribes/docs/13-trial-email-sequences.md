# 13 — Trial Email Sequences (the 7-day conversion engine)

> The trial only converts if the person gets a **real win inside the 7 days**. These sequences are engineered to force that win early, prove the breadth of value mid-trial, and make continuing feel obvious by day 7. Two sequences — one per tier (both tiers get the 7-day risk-free, no-obligation trial).

**Sender:** `Kingdom Scribes` (faceless — no personal name). **Voice:** bold, KJV-rooted, warm, no hype. **Every email:** one clear action, mobile-first, short.

**Universal mechanics:**
- Send **Day 0 within 2 minutes** of signup. Timing below is from signup moment.
- One primary call-to-action (CTA) button per email. Don't split attention.
- Reference the trial honestly: when it ends, that you can cancel anytime, no obligation.
- Push **annual** only on Day 6–7, once value is proven ("2 months free").
- Tag/segment by tier so a minister never gets seeker copy and vice-versa.

## After the trial — two short follow-ups

### If they CONTINUE → Day 8 onboarding (both tiers)
**Subject:** You're in — here's how to get the most out of it
A warm "glad you stayed," point them to the request line (ministers) / next track (seekers), and set the expectation of the monthly drop. This cements the habit that drives long-term retention ([04 — Retention](04-value-ladder-and-pricing.md)).

### If they CANCEL → Win-back (Day 1 and Day 14 after cancel)
**Subject (Day 1):** No hard feelings — one question
> Ask *why* (one-click survey: too busy / price / not what I needed / not the right time). The answers tune the funnel.
**Subject (Day 14):** A free study for you
> Send a genuinely useful free study (no pitch) to stay in relationship. Re-invite later when a new track or title launches. Keep them on the list; a "no for now" is not a "no forever."
