# 12 — Risks & Stewardship

> Every plan should name where it breaks. This one has two kinds of risk: **business risks** (where the model fails) and the **stewardship risk** (where growth tactics could compromise the mission). The second matters more.

## The stewardship line (the most important section in this plan)

Kingdom Scribes is a **ministry that funds itself**, not a business wearing a cross. Hormozi's tools are about delivering value and not wasting attention — that is good stewardship *until* it becomes manipulation or until money starts steering doctrine. Guardrails:

1. **The gospel is never paywalled.** The free Acts 2:38 Guide stays free. A benevolence/scholarship path keeps the Seeker Membership reachable for those who genuinely can't pay. We monetize **ministers, convenience, and physical goods** — not access to salvation.
2. **Doctrine never bends to the market.** The fastest way to grow would be to soften the Apostolic position and sell to all "Christians." **We will not.** The niche *is* the moat ([02](02-market-and-avatars.md)); compromise would destroy the one thing ministers trust us for.
3. **No manipulation dressed as marketing.** No fake scarcity, no fabricated testimonies, no bait-and-switch hiding the doctrine to widen reach. Only honest urgency, real proof, and guarantees we honor ([01](01-mission-and-doctrinal-guardrails.md)).
4. **Faceless stays faceless.** No drift toward a personality/guru brand, even though that often grows faster. The messenger decreases; the message increases.
5. **Money serves the mission, not vice-versa.** When a growth tactic and the mission conflict, the mission wins — every time, even at a revenue cost.

> Litmus test for any tactic: *"Would a sound Apostolic pastor be proud to see how we did this?"* If not, don't.

## Business risks & mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead flow stalls** (the current constraint) | High | High | One channel worked fully before adding others; warm outreach gives fast, controllable leads independent of algorithms ([06](06-lead-generation.md)) |
| **Doctrinal error slips into content** | Med | Severe (kills trust) | Mandatory doctrinal-review gate on *everything*; soundness guarantee with correction-and-credit ([08](08-content-engine-faceless-ops.md)) |
| **Churn eats growth** | Med | High | Day-one win, monthly drops, annual billing, dunning, community ([04](04-value-ladder-and-pricing.md)) |
| **Over-reliance on one platform** | Med | High | Own the email list + SEO/owned content; platforms are rented, the list is owned |
| **Niche perceived as "too small"** | Low | Med | Math shows single-digit penetration clears 7 figures; Church/multi-seat + Kids + global Apostolic expand the ceiling ([09](09-financial-model.md)) |
| **Founder/key-person bottleneck** (esp. doctrinal reviewer) | Med | High | Document SOPs; train a second qualified reviewer; faceless model already reduces personality dependence |
| **Paid ads scaled too early** | Med | Med | Hard rule: no ads until funnel proven & LTGP:CAC > 3:1 ([10](10-90-day-roadmap.md)) |
| **Kids unit economics thin** | Med | Low | Judge Kids on blended LTV incl. membership flywheel; POD removes inventory risk; bundles raise AOV ([05](05-kids-division.md)) |
| **Payment/processor risk** (religious content sometimes flagged) | Low | Med | Use processors comfortable with faith content; keep clean compliance; have a backup processor |
| **Copyright/licensing confusion** (editable "brandable" content) | Low | Med | Crystal-clear license terms: what ministers may/may not do; protect the library from redistribution outside license |

## Concentration & dependency watch-list

- **Email list = the crown jewel.** If every other channel vanished, the owned list + SEO content keep the business alive. Protect and grow it relentlessly.
- **The doctrinal reviewer is a single point of failure.** Document the review standard and train redundancy early.
- **Don't let one channel become 100% of leads.** Diversify only *after* the first channel is profitable — but do diversify.

## What success looks like (and the trap inside it)

Success = Kingdom Scribes becomes the **default Apostolic resource library** — the first place a pastor, teacher, seeker, or parent goes for sound, ready-to-use, KJV-grounded material.

**The trap:** at scale, the pressure to compromise *increases* (broaden the doctrine, paywall more, build a personality, cut the benevolence). The discipline that got you here — the niche, the gate, the faceless Word-centered brand, the free gospel — is the same discipline that must hold at $1M and $10M. **Guard the guardrails hardest when growth tempts you to drop them.**

## Final word

This plan applies aggressive, proven growth frameworks to a ministry. Used within the guardrails, that's faithful stewardship: getting the truth of Acts 2:38 to more seekers, equipping more ministers, and planting the Word in more children — efficiently, without waste, without compromise. *Things new and old* (Matthew 13:52): new methods, old truth. Keep them in that order.
