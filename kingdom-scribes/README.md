# Kingdom Scribes — Hormozi-Style Growth Plan

> *"Then said he unto them, Therefore every scribe which is instructed unto the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old."* — **Matthew 13:52 (KJV)**

A complete, deep, and exhaustive launch-and-growth plan for **TheKingdomScribes.com** (adult library + licensing) and **Kingdom Scribes Kids** — built on Alex Hormozi's frameworks from *$100M Offers* and *$100M Leads*, adapted to a KJV-only Apostolic content ministry.

This is **separate from the ASL app** in this repo. Nothing here imports, depends on, or touches the sign-language code. It is a standalone strategy workspace (markdown only — no app code).

---

## What Kingdom Scribes is (the premise this plan is built on)

A **KJV-only Apostolic content library and licensing platform** serving seekers, saints, ministers, pastors, Sunday-school teachers, and faith content creators with doctrinally firm Bible studies, devotionals, evangelism tools, sermon resources, and children's books.

- **Brand anchor:** Matthew 13:52 — a "scribe instructed unto the kingdom" bringing forth "things new and old."
- **Doctrinal position (non-negotiable):** Repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, receiving the Holy Ghost, holy living. Acts 2:38 is the spine.
- **Faceless by design:** Built around the Word and the doctrine, not a personality. A *ministry-resource library*, not an influencer brand.
- **Free front door:** The **Acts 2:38 Guide** (5-part) — the primary lead magnet.
- **Two paid paths (current):**
  - **Seeker Membership** — $12/mo or $97/yr (personal growth).
  - **Minister License** — $47/mo or $397/yr (editable, brandable content for pastors/teachers/creators).
- **Kids branch:** *Kingdom Scribes Kids* — KJV-grounded Apostolic books. Flagship: *The Day Everything Changed ("A Lydia Story")*, ages 3–5, built around Acts 2:38. Future series for ages 6–8 and 9–12.

> Give away the best free Apostolic salvation guide on the internet (Acts 2:38), convert seekers into a low-friction membership, and turn the *real* money on the **Minister License** — because every licensed minister is a distribution channel who multiplies the Word to a whole congregation.

## A note on tone

Hormozi's frameworks are about **clarity, value, and stewardship of attention** — getting the right message to the right people without waste. Applied here, the goal is not hype; it's to remove every obstacle between a seeker and the gospel, and between a minister and the tools to preach it. Every aggressive-sounding tactic in this plan is filtered through [the doctrinal guardrails](docs/01-mission-and-doctrinal-guardrails.md) and [the stewardship line](docs/12-risks-and-stewardship.md).
