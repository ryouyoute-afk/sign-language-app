# Start Here — Kingdom Scribes, Explained Simply

*No jargon. Just the plan, in plain words. Read this one page first.*

---

## What Kingdom Scribes is

A website that gives pastors, teachers, families, and seekers **sound Bible material** — sermons, lessons, devotionals, evangelism tools, and kids' books — all built on Acts 2:38 and the King James Bible. You don't put your face on it. The brand is the Word, not a person.

There are two sides: the **adult side** (TheKingdomScribes.com) and the **kids side** (Kingdom Scribes Kids).

---

## How it makes money — 3 doors

1. **Free door:** the Acts 2:38 Guide. Costs nothing. It's how strangers find you.
2. **$12/month door:** regular people who want to grow in the Word (Seeker Membership).
3. **$47/month door:** pastors and teachers who get content they can put their own church's name on (Minister License). **This door makes most of the money.**

Both paid doors have your **7-day free trial** — try it a week, cancel anytime, no obligation.

---

## The one big idea

> **Win the pastors.**

Here's why. When one pastor pays you $47/month, you didn't just get one customer. That pastor:
- hands your material to his **whole church** (more people reached),
- buys your **kids' books** for his families,
- and tells **other pastors** about you.

One pastor turns into many people. That's the secret. So your energy goes toward pastors first.

---

## What to do first (the only thing that's missing)

Your product is good. Your prices are fine. The **only** problem is: not enough people know you exist.

So the first job is simple — **get the word out.** That's it. Don't overthink it.

**This week, do these 5 things:**
1. Make sure your free Acts 2:38 Guide is easy to find and sign up for.
2. Make sure your Minister License page clearly shows the 7-day free trial.
3. **Personally message 20 Apostolic pastors** you know (or know of). Offer them free samples to try this Sunday.
4. Post a few short videos that answer salvation questions (like "Why be baptized in Jesus' name?"). End each one with: *"Get the free Acts 2:38 Guide."*
5. Write down your numbers each week (how many signed up, how many became members).

That's the whole machine, small. Everything else is just turning it up louder.

---

## The trick that makes the free trial work

A free trial only turns into a paying customer if the person **gets something useful in the first day or two.**

- For a **pastor:** the moment he signs up, get him to download one sermon, put his church's name on it, and preach it Sunday. If he uses it once, he won't cancel.
- For a **seeker:** get him to read one devotional the first day. If it feeds him once, he'll stay.

So the very first email after signup shouldn't say "look around." It should say "here's your first win — go."

## What you could earn (rough idea, not a promise)

If it goes slow: around **$150,000 a year.**
If it goes normal: around **$500,000 a year.**
If it goes great: around **$1,000,000+ a year.**

What decides which one? **How many pastors and churches sign up.** Everything points back to that.

## The one rule that matters most

This is a **ministry that pays for itself** — not a business wearing a cross.

- The gospel stays **free.** Nobody is ever turned away from salvation content because of money.
- The doctrine **never** gets watered down to sell to more people. Being clearly Apostolic is your strength, not a limit.
- No fake marketing tricks. Just honest offers and real help.

If growing ever means breaking one of these, **don't.** The mission wins.
