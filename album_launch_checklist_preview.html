🎵 Album Launch Checklist — Digital Platforms

A step-by-step guide for releasing a record album on Spotify, Apple Music, and other digital services.

1 Pick a Distributor

  • DistroKid — unlimited uploads, keep 100% royalties
  • TuneCore — per-release or subscription, detailed reporting
  • CD Baby — one-time fee per release
  • Amuse / UnitedMasters — free tier, good for starting out
  • AWAL / Symphonic — application-based, label-style support

2 Prepare Your Assets

  • Audio — mastered WAV (16-bit/44.1kHz min, 24-bit preferred)
  • Cover art — square, 3000×3000 px, no text / URLs
  • Metadata — titles, artists, genre, credits
  • ISRC codes (per track) + UPC/EAN (album) — distributor provides
  • Songwriter splits documented

3 Set a Release Date

  • Submit to distributor 3–4 weeks early
  • Pitch Spotify editorial playlists 7+ days before release
  • Target a Friday release

4 Claim Your Artist Profiles

  • Spotify for Artists
  • Apple Music for Artists

5 Register to Collect ALL Royalties

  • PRO — ASCAP, BMI, or SESAC (performance royalties)
  • Publishing administrator — e.g., Songtrust (mechanical/publishing)
  • SoundExchange — digital/satellite radio royalties

6 Promote Around Launch

  • Pre-save campaign
  • YouTube Content ID (via distributor)
  • TikTok / Instagram / Reels music libraries
  • Pitch independent playlist curators
  • Notify mailing list & socials
