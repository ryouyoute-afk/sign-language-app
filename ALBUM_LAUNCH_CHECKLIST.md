# 🎵 Album Launch Checklist — Digital Platforms

A practical, step-by-step guide for releasing a record album on Spotify, Apple Music, and other digital services.

---

## 1. Pick a Distributor

You don't upload directly to streaming services — you go through a distributor that delivers to all of them at once.

- [ ] Choose a distributor:
  - **DistroKid** — annual subscription, unlimited uploads, keep 100% royalties
  - **TuneCore** — per-release or subscription, detailed reporting
  - **CD Baby** — one-time fee per release, no recurring cost
  - **Amuse / UnitedMasters** — free tier available, good for starting out
  - **AWAL / Symphonic** — application-based, revenue share, label-style support
- [ ] Create your distributor account

## 2. Prepare Your Assets

- [ ] **Audio** — high-quality WAV (16-bit/44.1kHz minimum; 24-bit preferred), properly mastered
- [ ] **Cover art** — square, **3000×3000 px**, RGB, no URLs / social handles / pricing text
- [ ] **Metadata** — album title, track titles, artist + featured artists, genre, songwriter/producer credits
- [ ] **ISRC codes** — one per track (distributor usually generates these free)
- [ ] **UPC/EAN** — one for the album (distributor provides)
- [ ] **Songwriter splits** documented if you co-wrote anything

## 3. Set a Release Date

- [ ] Submit to your distributor **at least 3–4 weeks early**
- [ ] Pitch unreleased tracks to **Spotify editorial playlists** via Spotify for Artists (must be **7+ days before** release)
- [ ] Target a **Friday** release to align with the global new-music cycle

## 4. Claim Your Artist Profiles

- [ ] **Spotify for Artists** — claim profile, edit bio/photos, access analytics
- [ ] **Apple Music for Artists** — claim profile and analytics

## 5. Register to Collect ALL Royalties

Distribution only collects master/streaming royalties. Don't leave the rest on the table:

- [ ] Join a **PRO** (ASCAP, BMI, or SESAC in the US) — performance royalties
- [ ] Sign up with a **publishing administrator** (e.g., Songtrust) — mechanical/publishing royalties
- [ ] Register with **SoundExchange** — digital/satellite radio royalties (US)

## 6. Promote Around Launch

- [ ] Set up a **pre-save campaign** before release
- [ ] Configure **YouTube Content ID** (via distributor) to monetize uses of your music
- [ ] Add tracks to **TikTok / Instagram / Reels** music libraries
- [ ] Pitch to independent playlist curators
- [ ] Notify your mailing list and socials

---

## ⏱️ Quick Timeline

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **6+ weeks out** | Finish & master tracks, gather art + metadata |
| **4 weeks out** | Upload to distributor, set release date |
| **3+ weeks out** | Claim artist profiles, pitch Spotify editorial playlists |
| **1–2 weeks out** | Launch pre-save campaign, pitch curators |
| **Release day (Fri)** | Promote across socials & mailing list |
| **Ongoing** | Confirm PRO / publishing / SoundExchange registrations are collecting |
